About

Technology has reached a critical point compared to generations before. Machines now speak, hear, see, understand, and answer—disrupting existing jobs in both blue- and white-collar domains. We need creative thinking and problem-solving skills to navigate this environment. Our vision is to nurture technically grounded leaders, problem solvers, innovators, and entrepreneurs to make a positive impact.

We conduct face-to-face workshops to equip the youth in Sri Lanka with Design Thinking. These methods provide a proven protocol for progress in a highly competitive environment, igniting a transformative mindset for innovation.

Programs

DT Education

Interactive seminars for private and public sector professionals who want to learn design thinking fundamentals.

DT Guidance

One-day workshops for organisations and young adults to apply design thinking to address specific problems.

DT Bootcamp

Multi-day residential workshops focused on design innovation, where participants learn to identify and solve real-world problems with creative, human-centred approaches.

Our workshops are designed by Prof. Suranga Nanayakkara, who has extensive experience in developing and teaching Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Design Thinking courses internationally.
