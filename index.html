Vision

Nurturing the next generation of problem solvers

Technology has reached a critical point compared to generations before. Machines now speak, hear, see, understand, and answer—disrupting existing jobs in both blue- and white-collar domains. We need creative thinking and problem-solving skills to navigate this environment. Our vision is to nurture technically grounded leaders, problem solvers, innovators, and entrepreneurs to make a positive impact.

Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship (ICE) Bootcamp

What is ICE

A residential, 3-day intensive programme where 30 carefully selected students from across all universities work alongside expert international facilitators to go from an idea to a working prototype in just 40 hours.

ICE Bootcamp is modelled after the Stanford Design Thinking bootcamp, which costs USD 14,000 per person. We deliver it entirely free of charge for Sri Lankan students, supported by the Augmented Human Lab at NUS School of Computing and SHA Foundation.

“Our workshops are designed for real impact—grounded in years of teaching Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Design Thinking internationally.”

The Platform

Design Thinking @ SL — the ICE participant platform

Design Thinking @ SL is the web application behind our Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship (ICE) programmes. Approved participants, mentors, and organisers sign in with their Google account to build a profile, join a team, manage their project, upload intro and pitch videos, message teammates, and receive their programme @designthinking.lk account.

The platform uses Google Sign-In and Google Workspace to authenticate members, provision accounts, store project files in Google Drive, manage programme events, and send programme emails. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service to learn how your data is handled.
