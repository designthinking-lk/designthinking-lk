Design Thinking @ SL

Design Thinking @ SL is a web application that runs our Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship (ICE) programmes. Approved participants, mentors, and organisers sign in with their Google account to build a profile, form and join teams, manage their projects, upload their work, and receive a programme @designthinking.lk account.

What the app does

Once signed in with Google, members use Design Thinking @ SL to:

Create a member profile

Set up a profile with your name, photo, skills, and bio so teammates and mentors can find you.

Form and join teams

Join a programme team, see your teammates, and collaborate throughout the ICE bootcamp.

Manage projects

Create and update your project, add tools and resources, and track it from idea to prototype.

Upload your work

Upload project images and short intro and pitch videos, stored securely in Google Drive.

Message teammates

Post updates and message other members to coordinate your work during the programme.

Get a programme account

Approved participants are issued a managed @designthinking.lk Google Workspace account.

How the app uses Google Sign-In

Design Thinking @ SL uses Google Sign-In and Google Workspace only to run the programme. We request access to the following, and only for the purposes shown:

Google accessWhy the app needs it
Email & basic profileTo sign you in and show your name and photo in the platform.
Google DriveTo store the images and intro/pitch videos you upload and your project files.
Directory (Admin)To provision and manage @designthinking.lk accounts for approved members.
CalendarTo create and manage events for ICE programmes and sessions.
Send emailTo send invitations, account details, and programme notifications.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Our use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
